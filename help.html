Sheet Builder

  1. Screenshot the instructions to the clipboard: Cmd+Ctrl+Shift+4, drag.
  2. Paste into any sheet: Cmd+V.
  3. Press Cmd+Option+B, or Sheet Builder tab › Build from Screenshot.

You get an Answers sheet (model-solution layout, numbers linked to the model), the model sheet(s) with the key formulas highlighted green, any charts or decision trees, and an Instruction Bank sheet.

The Instruction Bank keeps what was read, the screenshot, assumptions and open questions. Fix misreadings, answer the questions in the yellow cells, paste extra notes or screenshots, then click Rebuild from Bank.
